Rules & guidelines
Scope of Work

We edit what you submit. We improve clarity, specificity, structure, and prompt compliance across personal statements, activities, secondaries, and more. We strengthen narrative logic, tighten language, and remove reader friction so your draft reads clean, credible, and intentional.

We do not fabricate, exaggerate, or invent experiences. If a claim reads inflated or unverifiable, we flag it and rewrite it into something true and strong. We do not submit anything on your behalf, contact schools, or guarantee outcomes. Your job is to submit accurate inputs. Our job is to return a draft package that is easier to revise and harder to misread.

Email Thread Rules

Use email for questions and clarifications, not for resubmissions. We answer questions about your edits and how to apply the revision map inside your email thread. Keep questions specific and paste the exact sentence or paragraph you are asking about so we can respond quickly.

Resubmissions happen through the intake form. If your tier includes a resubmission, submit your revised draft through the intake form again within your tier window. Communication is exclusively via email. Phone and video calls are not included.

What You Submit (60,000 char.)

Your tier includes a 60,000 character bank valid for the full cycle. Submit your Primary and all Secondaries in batches as you finish them. We track your remaining balance with every delivery. Paste the prompt for each piece, your current draft, the character or word limits, and any hard constraints such as schools, tone, topics to avoid, and must-keep details. If you submit multiple documents, label each file clearly.

Use the same email you used at checkout. We verify eligibility by matching your intake email to the purchase record. If they do not match, we pause your order until you confirm which email is correct.

One batch equals one turnaround cycle. You may submit any number of documents per batch as long as you stay within your remaining character balance. Each batch takes 2 to 4 days to return. Your 30 Flex Support days are shared across the full cycle, so you can save characters for later and pace your submissions from your primary application all the way through your final secondary submission.
Privacy & Integrity

Your drafts are confidential. We treat your materials as private client documents and store only what we need to deliver the service. If you want deletion after delivery or after your questions window ends, reply in the thread and we will confirm once it is done.

Integrity rules are strict. We will not assist with fabrication, fake metrics, invented roles, or experiences you did not live. We will flag any content that creates credibility risk and fix it by improving specificity, context, and restraint.

Resubmissions (unlimited included)

Resubmissions follow one rule: revise first, then resend. After you receive your packet, apply the revision map and edits before sending a new draft. When you resubmit, include a short note telling us what changed and what you want checked next. This prevents loop edits.

Your tier includes unlimited resubmissions. These are available anytime your Flex Support balance is active. Use them for ongoing questions and additional feedback on every draft you submit throughout the cycle.
Timeline / Delivery

We start after your intake is complete. Your turnaround clock of 2 to 4 days begins when we have a complete intake submission including prompt, draft, and limits, and your email matches the purchase record. If anything is missing, we pause until it is corrected.

Unlimited includes 30 Flex Support days. These 30 days do not need to be used consecutively. The clock only ticks on days when we are actively reviewing your drafts or answering questions, ensuring support from your primary application through your final secondary submission. Your profile closes when your Flex Support days are exhausted.

Deep Revisions (3 included)

Deep Revision Cycles are credibility-verified review passes, not our typical edits. Each cycle includes a primary editor pass for structure and clarity, a second reviewer pass for narrative logic and prompt fit, and a senior reviewer pass focused on credibility, signal strength, and avoidable red flags. You receive one consolidated PDF packet with aligned edits and a refreshed revision map.

Your tier includes 3 Deep Revision Cycles within the 30-day Flex Support window. We will tell you when your draft needs a deep cycle versus a standard resubmission so you do not use a cycle on small tweaks.
